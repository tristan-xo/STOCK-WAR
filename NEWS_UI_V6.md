# Stock Wars News UI V6

The news feed now follows a finance-newspaper-inspired layout based on the supplied reference:
- Large numbered stories
- Strong headline hierarchy
- Thin editorial divider
- Category, sector and sentiment metadata
- 3–5 line story body
- Expected market impact
- Five stories fit in a normal desktop viewport; the remaining stories continue in the same scrollable bulletin.
- The same visual language is used in player and host news sections.
- Mobile switches to a compact single-column layout.

The underlying news generation and price-impact logic is unchanged.
