# Stock Wars Phase 4

Adds the competition/finale layer:
- Stable Player IDs: STW-001...
- Printable QR player cards
- Advanced rankings with tie-break rules
- Sector performance analytics
- Round trading analytics
- Finale / winner spotlight
- Presentation Mode control
- Final results screen for players after Round 6
- Printable certificates
- Final result export remains available

Run `npm install` after extraction so the new `qrcode` dependency is installed.
